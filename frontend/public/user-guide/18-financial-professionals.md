# 18. Letting a financial professional in

[← Letting a client in](16-client-portal.md) · [Index](README.md)

---

A tracing expert, a CPA or a forensic accountant working on a case can be given
access to that matter's **Accounts**, **Transactions** and **Inventory** —
nothing else — once the client has consented. They can read, export, and tag
transactions with tags of their own. They cannot change anything.

Everything is on the matter's detail page, under **Financial professionals**.

## What they can and cannot do

| They can | They cannot |
| -------- | ----------- |
| See every account, with its ownership and characterization | See account notes or purpose |
| See statements, open the statement PDFs, read every line | Add, edit, delete, merge or re-import anything |
| Search transactions and filter by category | Assign a category |
| Create their own tags and apply them | See your tags, the client's tags, or another professional's |
| See the inventory, including exclusions and conversions | Exclude an account or set a rate |
| Export transactions and the inventory, in all four formats | See pleadings, claims, discovery, billing, the FIS, the compliance grid, the undisclosed-accounts report, or any other matter |

Their exports say **"Prepared for [their name]"** at the foot of every page.

## Giving access

1. Open the matter and find **Financial professionals**. Click **Give a
   professional access**.
2. Enter their name and **the email address they will sign in with**, their
   organization and profession, what they were retained to do, and how long
   access lasts (180 days unless you change it).
3. Choose the **designation**: *Testifying expert*, *Consulting expert*, or
   *Non-expert* (an accountant or advisor helping the client, not retained as an
   expert). You can change it later; the change is recorded.
4. Click **Add**. The row reads **awaiting consent**. **Nothing is visible to
   them yet.**

An address that already signs in to Cyclone as staff or as a client is refused.
Use a different address for guest access.

## Recording the client's consent

When the client agrees, click **Record consent** on the row. Choose how they
consented (by phone, in person, by email, by letter), the date, and a note if
there is anything worth keeping. Click **Record and open access**.

The record names **you** as the person attesting. The professional is emailed
straight away that the client has consented, with a reminder that their access
exists to use the transaction database on the client's behalf and solely for
the client's benefit. The same reminder sits at the top of every screen they
see.

They sign in at the usual login page with that email address, with Google or a
link sent to their email. They see only the matters they have been given.

## Renewing, changing and withdrawing access

- **Renew** sets the access to run for a number of days from today. When the
  date passes, the row reads **expired** and the professional sees nothing.
  Nothing has to run for that to happen.
- **Revoke** withdraws access immediately, on their next click. A dialog asks
  for an optional reason and offers three buttons: **Cancel**, **Withdraw from
  this matter**, and — set apart, in red — **Withdraw from every matter**.
- A revoked professional can be given access again. That is a new grant, and it
  needs consent again.

## What they were provided

For a testifying expert, what they were provided is disclosable. Click
**Documents provided** on the row for the list: every statement they had access
to, with its account, period, filename and Bates range, and the date it became
available. Export it in any of the four formats.

**A statement deleted after they had access stays on the list**, greyed, with
the date it was removed. Rejecting a statement deletes it outright, so without
this list the record of what the expert saw would quietly shrink.

The list records documents, not activity: Cyclone does not keep a record of what
they searched for or which lines they looked at. It does record when they
opened the app, which shows whether access was used after it was withdrawn.

## Their tags, on your screens

A professional's tags appear on your **Transactions** tab marked **◆** with
their name. You can filter on them to see what the expert flagged. You cannot
apply, edit or delete them: they are the expert's own assertions. And one
professional never sees another's tags, even on the same matter, so a
consulting expert's work never reaches a testifying expert this way.
